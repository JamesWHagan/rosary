# Decisions

A record of choices made for Decade4U, so contributors know what was decided and why.

## 2026-10-13

- **Name:** Decade4U, because each person prays one decade for another person (renamed from Rosary2U)
- **First language:** English. Italian and Sinhala are planned later.
- **Rosary structure:**
  - Opening: Sign of the Cross, Apostles' Creed, Our Father, three Hail Marys, Glory Be, **Fatima prayer** (added 2026-10-14)
  - Each decade: announce the mystery and the intention, Our Father, ten Hail Marys, Glory Be, **Fatima prayer**
  - Closing: **Hail Holy Queen**, then the **closing prayer** ("O God, whose only begotten Son…"), Sign of the Cross
  - Mysteries follow the traditional weekday: Joyful on Monday and Saturday, Sorrowful on Tuesday and Friday, Glorious on Wednesday and Sunday, Luminous on Thursday
- **Licence:** GNU AGPL-3.0, so every version of the project stays open
- **Hosting:** Netlify, deploying automatically from the `main` branch (moved to Cloudflare Pages on 2026-10-14, see below)
- **Look:** calm and warm. White beads with a soft yellow glow on the active bead, a serif heading font (Cormorant Garamond), and a light or dark theme that follows the phone's own setting
- **Bead direction:** beads are counted clockwise, going up the right-hand side from the medal
- **Two ways to pray:** "Bead by bead" (every prayer on screen, 80 steps; tap the card or Next) and "With my own rosary" (one screen per decade, 7 steps) for people praying on a physical rosary
- **Choosing decades:** people pray 1–5 decades, and each decade is assigned one other person's intention. Fewer than 5 decades uses the short version (just the Sign of the Cross before and after); 5 decades uses the full opening and closing prayers. Partial rosaries start from the first mystery of the day.
- **Finish screen:** shows each intention prayed for, with that person's thank-you message (or a warm default)

## 2026-10-14

- **Hide prayers:** a switch on the prayer screen hides the prayer texts. The rosary then fills the screen, the current prayer and count show inside the loop, and the intention stays visible in a strip below. The choice is remembered.
- **Touching the rosary:** in bead-by-bead mode, tapping the rosary or the prayer moves to the next bead, with a soft ripple and a light vibration on phones that support it (stronger at the end of a decade). Swipe left for next and right for back, anywhere on the prayer screen, in both modes. While the prayers are hidden, the Back and Next buttons are hidden too, and a one-line hint explains the gestures until the person has moved on a few times.
- **Partial rosaries:** beads of decades not being prayed are faded.
- **Decade panel:** on every bead of a decade, a panel under the rosary shows the mystery being meditated on and the intention being prayed for, with the prayers hidden or shown. (With prayers shown, the panel is skipped on the mystery screen itself, which already shows both.)
- **"People you have prayed for" counter:** a golden ring with a circling arc, on the home page and the finish screen. It counts finished decades on the person's own phone (each decade is one person). A decade counts only when its last prayer is passed, never twice. The ring is hidden until the first decade, so no one sees a zero.
- **Planned (Phase 4):** a "My intentions" page with the number of people who prayed for each intention, plus a second golden ring for the total prayed for you. Waiting intentions say "Waiting to be prayed for" instead of 0. About 3 active intentions per person.
- **Planned (Phase 6):** "Someone is praying for you", shown on the site and as an optional notification that people can turn off. Batched (for example at most one per few hours) so it stays special, and it never names who prayed.
- **Wording:** the site says an intention has been **prayed for**, never **answered**. How a prayer is answered is up to God, so we only promise what we can: that people prayed.
- **Keeping every intention prayed for (planned):** least-prayed intentions go first; after submitting, people are invited (never required) to pray one decade for someone else; the admin page shows the balance (intentions waiting, decades prayed today, oldest waiting) so a backlog is visible early; if one builds, the home page says how many intentions are waiting.
- **"… need your prayers" on the home page:** each time the home page opens it features up to 3 people whose intentions nobody has prayed for yet, chosen at random and avoiding the three shown last time. Anonymous ones read naturally ("Maria, Luca and one more person need your prayers"). "Pray for them" starts a rosary with exactly those people, one decade each. If nobody is waiting, it says "Everyone has been prayed for. Thank you 🙏".
- **js/intentions.js** is the single place the site gets intentions from (sample list for now), so connecting the database in Phase 4 only changes that file. Intentions are handed out least-prayed-first.
- **Home page = the first thing everyone sees.** Order from the top: the Decade4U title with the tagline "One decade. One person. One prayer." and a short explanation; the "… need your prayers" card as the main action; the golden "people you have prayed for" ring; then "Choose how many decades"; and a small "Free forever · Open source" footer. A soft golden glow sits behind the title. The page scrolls normally, so the title is never cut off on small screens.
- **Hosting moved to Cloudflare Pages** at decade4u.pages.dev. Netlify's free plan charges credits per deploy and per visitor and pauses the site when they run out; Cloudflare Pages is free with unlimited visitors and 500 deploys a month. Rule for costs: only free plans that pause rather than bill, no card on pay-as-you-go plans, and a spending cap before any upgrade.
- **Domain:** decade4u.com, bought through Cloudflare Registrar ($10.46/year, auto-renew on). Chosen over .org because .com is what most people type, so it is the easiest to find. www.decade4u.com and decade4u.pages.dev also lead to the site.
- **Updates reach phones immediately:** `_headers` sets `Cache-Control: no-cache` on every file, and the decade4u.com Cloudflare setting "Browser Cache TTL" is set to "Respect Existing Headers" (the default of 4 hours made phones show old versions after updates).
- **Desktop layout:** on screens 900px and wider the same pages rearrange side by side (home: title and counter left, people who need prayers right; setup: choices left, people right; prayer: big rosary left, mystery/intention/prayer right; Amen: thank-you messages in a grid). Phones keep the single-column layout. On computers the gesture hint mentions clicking and the arrow keys instead of swiping.
- **Share your intention (test page, submit.html):** intention up to 300 characters; name shown as "Someone" by default, or a first name only, or a description ("A mother", "A student"…); optional thank-you up to 200 characters (warm default if empty). Intentions with an email, phone number or link are refused with a gentle explanation, to protect privacy. If an intention mentions suicide or self-harm (English or Italian), a "You are not alone" panel shows 112 and findahelpline.com; submitting is still allowed. The thank-you screen invites (never requires) praying one decade while waiting. Until the database exists, the page is not linked from the home page, says clearly that nothing is sent, and keeps the intention on the device only.
- **Database: Cloudflare D1** (free, same account as the website, stored in Western Europe, blocks rather than bills at its limits, never pauses for inactivity), instead of Supabase.
- **AI moderation from launch day.** The site must cope with a sudden crowd even when the maintainer is busy. Every new intention is checked within seconds by Cloudflare Workers AI (Llama Guard for harmful content, plus a small model asked whether it is a sincere prayer intention, contains identifying details, or sounds like a crisis). Clearly fine → approved automatically; unsure or crisis → the maintainer's queue; clearly abusive → refused with a gentle explanation. If the free daily AI allowance (10,000 neurons, roughly 400 intentions) runs out, new intentions wait in the queue; nothing is ever approved unchecked. Safety nets: Report button, a daily look at what the AI approved, Turnstile, per-device limits, and an email to the maintainer when the queue grows.
- **General intentions instead of invented people:** when there are fewer waiting intentions than decades chosen, the extra decades are prayed for general intentions (everyone who asked for prayer on Decade4U, the sick and dying, the souls in Purgatory, peace, those with no one to pray for them). The live site never shows invented people; sample intentions are only used when testing from a file.
- **AI moderation results (tested on Cloudflare, 14 Oct):** sincere intentions in English and Italian approved automatically; full names/addresses and anything the safety model is unsure about go to the queue; crisis wording goes to the queue marked for care; adverts, mockery and clearly harmful content are refused with a gentle message.
- **Not live yet:** the connected version is on the `next` branch (next.decade4u.pages.dev). It goes live once the admin page exists, so that queued and crisis intentions are always seen by a person.
- **Swipe up/down:** on the prayer screen, swiping up shows the prayers and swiping down hides them (anywhere except on the prayer text, which scrolls). Pull-to-refresh is disabled while praying so a downward swipe never reloads the page.
- **Phones, prayers shown:** the intention in the decade panel may wrap to three lines and the rosary is a little smaller, so the full intention stays visible for people who always pray with the prayers shown.

## 2026-10-15

- **Admin page (admin.html):** protected by a password kept as a Cloudflare secret (`ADMIN_PASSWORD`, at least 12 characters), never in the code. The password is kept only for the browser tab. After 10 wrong passwords in an hour, that network address must wait an hour. (Cloudflare Access was considered, but its sign-up can ask for a payment card; a strong password is free and enough for one maintainer.)
- **What the admin page shows:** the balance (waiting for review, may be in crisis, approved but not yet prayed for, longest wait, decades prayed today, shared today, totals); **Waiting for you** (Approve / Reject; possible crisis first, with an orange border); **Reported** (Put back / Remove); **Approved** in the last 2 days (Hide), for a daily look at what the AI approved. Every decision is added to the intention's moderation notes.
- **Crisis intentions:** the person sharing always sees crisis resources. The intention then waits for the maintainer, who approves it if it is a sincere request for prayer that is fine for strangers to read, and rejects it if it is too explicit or detailed. Nothing marked as a possible crisis is ever approved automatically.
- **Report button:** on the "You will pray for" list and on the Amen screen. It asks once more ("Hide this intention and send it for review?"), then hides the intention for everyone at once and sends it to the Reported tab. On the setup screen a replacement intention is fetched. A device can send up to 10 reports a day. An intention that is put back starts with no reports.
- **Live:** the connected version (sharing, AI check, admin page) goes live on decade4u.com once the admin password is set in Cloudflare.
- **Intention lifetime:** an intention completes after 33 decades (one for each year of Jesus' life) or 30 days after approval, whichever comes first, but never before it has been prayed for at least once. Storage is not the reason (a million intentions would be about 0.5 GB of the free 5 GB): it keeps the pool fresh and frees a place so people can share a new intention (3 active at a time).
- **My intentions (mine.html):** shows each intention shared from this browser with its status (Being checked, Waiting to be prayed for, Being prayed for, Being reviewed, Complete), how many people have prayed for it, and a golden ring with the total who have prayed for you. Linked from the home page once you have shared one, and from the thank-you screen. Rejected intentions are not listed (rejection stays silent). Intentions are tied to the browser, not an account.
- **General intentions:** 31 of them, chosen at random to fill decades when there aren't enough people's intentions (common in the first days): the Pope and the Church, bishops and priests, vocations, the souls in Purgatory (one for each of the seven deadly sins, and those with no one to pray for them), the lukewarm, non-believers, those who left the Church, the wars in the world, refugees, newborns, expectant mothers and unborn babies, young people struggling with sexual temptation and impurity, families, persecuted Christians, the sick and dying, addiction, despair, the poor, the conversion of sinners.
